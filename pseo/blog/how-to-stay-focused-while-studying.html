The studying version of “stay focused”

“How to stay focused while studying” is not a mystery technique. Keywords Everywhere shows about 590 US searches/month for that phrase, plus another ~590 for “how to stay focused at work.” Same mechanic: protect a block, recover, repeat. Students just name it studying.

You already have a study timer spoke. This post is the problem playbook: what to do when the chapter is open and your brain is on another planet.

Three leaks that kill study sessions

  • Phone in reach — you do not have a willpower problem; you have a proximity problem.
  • YouTube “study with me” plus recommendations — ~4,400 searches/month for study-with-me is real, but the sidebar is how sessions die. Superfocus keeps countdown + optional sound in one tab.
  • No retrieval — rereading for 25 minutes feels focused and teaches little. Alternate timer blocks: input, then quiz yourself.

A Superfocus study night

  1. Sprint: gather notes / open the problem set. Low dignity, high activation.
  2. Two or three Pomodoros: one topic only. Name it on the task line (“chem ch. 4 problems 1–8”).
  3. Cassette: lofi or rain if the dorm is loud. Keywords Everywhere also shows heavy demand for “lofi study music” and “white noise for studying”—use cassettes or Spotify inside the timer so you are not shopping streams.
  4. Breaks away from the feed. Walk, water, not “one reel.”
  5. Last block: retrieval (closed-book questions) not more highlighting.

If 25 minutes still won't stick

Drop to Sprint. Or study in a library where the social cost of unlocking Instagram is higher. The timer is the cadence; the room is half the product.

Workdays swap “chapter” for “deliverable” and use the same stack—see work timer. The stay-focused-at-work query wants mute chat, one outcome, and a preset that matches energy, not a different philosophy.

Weekly, not nightly, scoreboard

Count finished Superfocus sessions for the week. If you only completed two, the issue is start friction (read task paralysis), not “I need a new aesthetic study setup.”
