Not medical advice—practical scaffolding

This is not a treatment guide. “How to focus with ADHD” is a huge search (about 1,600 US searches/month in Keywords Everywhere) because people need external structure: visible time, short starts, fewer decisions mid-block. Superfocus is that kind of scaffold—a browser timer with Sprint, Pomodoro, and ambient sound—not a clinic.

Why rigid 25s often fail

Classic Pomodoro assumes you can sit down for 25 on command. For a lot of ADHD days, 25 is already a wall. That is why Superfocus ships Sprint (short, ugly start) alongside Pomodoro, Flow, and Deep Work. The point is a timer you will obey today, not a tomato you abandon by 9:12.

Related keyword demand is real but smaller: “pomodoro technique adhd” (~480), “adhd timer app” (~90), “pomodoro for adhd” (~70). The job-to-be-done is the same: start, stay in one task, stop on purpose.

A Superfocus setup that respects variable energy

  1. Name the smallest next physical action (“open the doc,” not “finish the paper”).
  2. If activation is frozen, start Sprint. If you can already sit, Pomodoro.
  3. Turn on one cassette (lofi, rain, cafe, white noise). Do not shop playlists inside the block.
  4. Keep the task list to what this session will touch. Superfocus tasks are for the block, not a second life-admin app—unless you already live in Todoist + Pomodoro.

Rules that help more than motivation speeches

  • One tab for the work, one for the timer. Not a third for “research.”
  • Breaks are not optional. ADHD burnout often looks like “I finally started so I cannot stop.”
  • Restart without shame. A broken pomodoro is data. Start Sprint again.
  • Body doubling is a different product. Live coworking (Focusmate-style) can help; Superfocus is the solo container when you do not want a stranger on camera.

What Superfocus will not do

It will not replace medication, coaching, or sleep. It will not body-double you. It will make time visible and make “I'll start later” expire. For many people that is the missing piece of ADHD time management at a desk.
