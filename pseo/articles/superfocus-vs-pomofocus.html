Direct answer

This is a decision page, not a feature dump. Pomofocus is a fast 25/5 tomato in a browser tab. Superfocus is a browser pomodoro / focus timer billed at $1.99/month: one countdown, one named task, and optional cassette sound in the same tab. There is no Superfocus free plan and no guest timer.

  • You only want 25 on / 5 off — stay on Pomofocus. It is a well-known kitchen-timer page. Keep it if you already run music and tasks somewhere else and that split works.
  • You want the clock, a named task, and optional sound together — Superfocus. Premium is $1.99/month after a 7-day trial. Subscribe, then use the timer at /app.
  • You only need a countdown — a kitchen timer or the phone clock is $0. Do not pay $1.99/month for that.

Keep Pomofocus when 25/5 is the whole product

Pomofocus won because it looks like a kitchen timer in a browser. If you already have Spotify elsewhere and a task list elsewhere, and you like that split, stay. Switching tools for sport is not focus.

Choose Superfocus when that split is why sessions die: you open lofi on YouTube, the sidebar recommends a video, and the pomodoro never starts. Superfocus keeps the 25/5 default and adds longer presets, sound that is not a second destination, and a log of blocks you actually finished — billed at $1.99/month, not as a “free timer plus three other subscriptions.”

What Superfocus actually is

Superfocus is a browser pomodoro / focus timer: a clock, one named task, optional cassette sound (lofi, rain, and similar beds), Todoist if you already live there, and completed-session stats. Presets include Pomodoro 25/5, Sprint, Flow, and Deep Work.

It is not a guest timer. It is not a free plan with a daily cap. It is not “one free browser app.” Premium is $1.99/month after a 7-day trial.

Who should not subscribe

Do not pay $1.99/month if you want a $0 tomato and will never use sound, a named task, or history. Use Pomofocus or a kitchen timer. Do not pick Superfocus as a Forest clone — we do not grow trees. Pick Superfocus when you already work in a browser and you are tired of a timer tab, a music tab, and a task tab fighting each other.
