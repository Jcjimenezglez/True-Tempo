What is a ADHD focus timer?

Superfocus is a focus timer app that supports ADHD focus timer. Use Sprint (15/3/10 min) plus ambient sounds (lofi, rain, cafe), task tracking, and analytics. Free to try.

Who needs a ADHD focus timer?

Anyone who keeps “starting” ADHD focus timer sessions and somehow ends up in inbox, chat, or social. A dedicated ADHD focus timer makes the block explicit: one outcome, one countdown, then a break you actually take.

It is also useful when your environment is noisy or your calendar is fragmented—short presets between meetings, longer ones when you finally get a quiet hour.

Why ADHD focus timer matters for real work

A ADHD focus timer only helps if the session has a finish line. “Be productive” is not a finish line. “Draft section two” or “clear the review queue” is.

Use Sprint (15/3/10 min) when you want a known rhythm. If you burn out mid-block, drop to Sprint for a day—momentum beats heroic lengths you abandon.

Ambient sound is optional, but it is useful when your environment is unpredictable. Pair the same cassette with the same preset for a week so your brain learns the cue.

How to use a ADHD focus timer?

  1. Set up your ADHD focus timer session with the Sprint (15/3/10 min) preset
  2. Optionally start ambient sound to reduce distractions
  3. Work until the timer ends, then take the break
  4. Log the session and queue the next block

Common mistakes with ADHD focus timer

  • Multitasking inside the block — if the timer is running, one task owns it.
  • Skipping breaks — breaks are part of the method, not a reward you earn by suffering.
  • Perfect length obsession — pick Sprint (15/3/10 min), collect a week of data, then adjust.
  • Timer in one tab, distractions in five others — keep sound and tasks with the countdown when you can.
