What is a marathon timer?

A marathon timer uses 60-minute focus blocks for extended deep work. Superfocus Marathon preset (60/10/30) is built for writers, researchers, and anyone who needs longer uninterrupted sessions.

How this method differs from classic Pomodoro

Classic Pomodoro uses fixed 25/5 cycles. marathon focus sessions adjusts block length or break rules so the timer matches attention span and task type—without abandoning structure entirely.

Superfocus keeps multiple presets in one place so you can switch methods when 25 minutes is too short or too long.

Who should use marathon focus sessions?

Use this method when your default work style needs a clearer rhythm—too many open loops, or blocks that drag without a finish line. marathon focus sessions is especially useful if classic “work until done” turns into half-attention and late nights.

Skip it only when your task truly needs unbroken multi-hour immersion and you already protect that time. Even then, Superfocus Deep Work or Marathon presets may fit better than forcing a mismatched length.

Why marathon focus sessions matters for real work

marathon focus sessions is not a personality test—it is a constraint. The constraint is what creates finishing energy. When people say the method “doesn’t work,” they often mean they kept negotiating with the timer, restarted after every ping, or never protected the break.

How to use marathon focus sessions?

  1. Choose the marathon focus sessions approach (or the Marathon (60/10/30 min) preset)
  2. Add one task and start the focus block
  3. Take the recommended break, then repeat
  4. After a few rounds, review what length actually worked

Common mistakes with marathon focus sessions

  • Multitasking inside the block — if the timer is running, one task owns it.
  • Skipping breaks — breaks are part of the method, not a reward you earn by suffering.
  • Perfect length obsession — pick Marathon (60/10/30 min), collect a week of data, then adjust.
  • Timer in one tab, distractions in five others — keep sound and tasks with the countdown when you can.
