What is the Flowtime method?

The Flowtime method uses longer work blocks (45+ minutes) with flexible breaks. Superfocus offers the Flow preset (45/8/25) for those who prefer fewer interruptions and longer focus sessions. It's ideal for deep work and flow state.

How this method differs from classic Pomodoro

Classic Pomodoro uses fixed 25/5 cycles. flowtime method adjusts block length or break rules so the timer matches attention span and task type—without abandoning structure entirely.

Superfocus keeps multiple presets in one place so you can switch methods when 25 minutes is too short or too long.

Who should use flowtime method?

Use this method when your default work style needs a clearer rhythm—too many open loops, or blocks that drag without a finish line. flowtime method is especially useful if classic “work until done” turns into half-attention and late nights.

Skip it only when your task truly needs unbroken multi-hour immersion and you already protect that time. Even then, Superfocus Deep Work or Marathon presets may fit better than forcing a mismatched length.

Why flowtime method matters for real work

flowtime method is not a personality test—it is a constraint. The constraint is what creates finishing energy. When people say the method “doesn’t work,” they often mean they kept negotiating with the timer, restarted after every ping, or never protected the break.

In Superfocus, treat the Flow (45/8/25 min) preset as the default experiment. Run four cycles on one meaningful task. Only then decide whether you need shorter Sprint blocks or longer Flow/Deep Work time.

How to use flowtime method?

  1. Choose the flowtime method approach (or the Flow (45/8/25 min) preset)
  2. Add one task and start the focus block
  3. Take the recommended break, then repeat
  4. After a few rounds, review what length actually worked

Common mistakes with flowtime method

  • Multitasking inside the block — if the timer is running, one task owns it.
  • Skipping breaks — breaks are part of the method, not a reward you earn by suffering.
  • Perfect length obsession — pick Flow (45/8/25 min), collect a week of data, then adjust.
  • Timer in one tab, distractions in five others — keep sound and tasks with the countdown when you can.
