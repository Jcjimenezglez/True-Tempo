What is the 52-minute focus method?

The 52-minute focus method is based on ultradian rhythms. Work 52 minutes, break 17. Superfocus lets you create custom timers to match your body's natural cycles.

How this method differs from classic Pomodoro

Classic Pomodoro uses fixed 25/5 cycles. 52 minute focus adjusts block length or break rules so the timer matches attention span and task type—without abandoning structure entirely.

Superfocus keeps multiple presets in one place so you can switch methods when 25 minutes is too short or too long.

Who should use 52 minute focus?

Use this method when your default work style needs a clearer rhythm—too many open loops, or blocks that drag without a finish line. 52 minute focus is especially useful if classic “work until done” turns into half-attention and late nights.

Skip it only when your task truly needs unbroken multi-hour immersion and you already protect that time. Even then, Superfocus Deep Work or Marathon presets may fit better than forcing a mismatched length.

Why 52 minute focus matters for real work

52 minute focus is not a personality test—it is a constraint. The constraint is what creates finishing energy. When people say the method “doesn’t work,” they often mean they kept negotiating with the timer, restarted after every ping, or never protected the break.

How to use 52 minute focus?

  1. Choose the 52 minute focus approach (or the Custom 52/17 (create in app) preset)
  2. Add one task and start the focus block
  3. Take the recommended break, then repeat
  4. After a few rounds, review what length actually worked

Common mistakes with 52 minute focus

  • Multitasking inside the block — if the timer is running, one task owns it.
  • Skipping breaks — breaks are part of the method, not a reward you earn by suffering.
  • Perfect length obsession — pick Custom 52/17 (create in app), collect a week of data, then adjust.
  • Timer in one tab, distractions in five others — keep sound and tasks with the countdown when you can.
