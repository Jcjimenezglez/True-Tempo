What is a focus music?

Focus music (lofi, rain, cafe) helps block noise and signal your brain to concentrate. Superfocus includes built-in cassettes plus Spotify integration—use any timer preset with ambient sound.

Using focus music

Combine focus music with a Pomodoro or Flow preset so audio and timer stay synchronized. Start the cassette once per session—changing tracks mid-block breaks focus.

When focus music helps (and when it does not)

focus music works best as a consistent backdrop—steady enough to mask interruptions without stealing attention. If lyrics pull you into the song, switch to rain, cafe, or instrumental lofi inside Superfocus.

Why focus music matters for real work

focus music should support attention, not become another playlist rabbit hole. Pick one cassette, start the timer, and leave the library alone until the break.
